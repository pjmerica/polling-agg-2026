# Election-night curves by state (NYT timelines)

Each cell: **% of the final vote counted / margin of the counted vote** at N hours after the state's main poll close. Margin = top non-Republican minus top Republican, points of all votes (+ = Democrat/independent ahead). *Shift* = final margin minus the margin at 2 hours after close: positive means the count moved toward the non-Republican after that point.

Poll close used (ET): GA 7:00, OH 7:30, MI/TX/KS/ME 8:00, IA/NE 9:00, AK midnight. Built by `scripts/state_curves.py` from `data/raw/nyt` (2020, 2022, 2024).

Caveats:

- 2024 **president** timelines cover only 8 p.m.-5:40 a.m. ET (NYT model window); later cells repeat the last point. 2024 Senate files run through certification.
- 2020 timelines are Edison data; the vote-share fields are rounded to 0.1 pt.
- Alaska 2022 Senate: both leading candidates are Republican (Murkowski, Tshibaka), so the margin column is the Democrat vs the top Republican and says nothing about that race.
- Maine 2024 Senate: the non-Republican is King (I). Nebraska 2024 Senate: Osborn (I).
- Rules changed since these years (OH grace period ended, GA early votes due by 8 p.m., MI early tabulation from 2024): see the report.
