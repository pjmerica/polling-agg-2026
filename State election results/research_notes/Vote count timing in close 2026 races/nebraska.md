# Nebraska: vote counting, reporting order and timing for the Nov 3, 2026 general (U.S. Senate: Ricketts vs. Osborn)

Researched 2026-10-21. Primary-source statute text came from nebraskalegislature.gov. Several news pages (Nebraska Examiner, Lancaster County PDFs) returned HTTP 403. Facts taken only from search-result summaries of those pages are marked **[snippet]** and are less reliable than fetched pages.

## 1. 2026 rules: race field, poll hours, early-ballot deadlines and processing, ID, provisionals, canvass, recount

### Takeaway
Every Nebraska polling place closes at the same moment: **9:00 p.m. ET** (8 p.m. CT / 7 p.m. MT). The prompt guessed 9pm/10pm ET; that is wrong. Early ballots can be verified from Friday Oct 23, 2026 and tabulated from 24 hours before polls open. No results may be released before polls close, and early ballots must arrive by close of polls. The state canvass is Mon **Nov 30, 2026**. A recount is mandatory when the margin is 1% or less of the leader's votes. A statewide recount would be held Wed **Dec 2, 2026**.

**Poll hours:**
- Neb. Rev. Stat. 32-908(1): Central zone polls open 8 a.m. and close 8 p.m.; Mountain zone polls open 7 a.m. and close 7 p.m. — [32-908](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-908)
- The SoS voter reminder says polls close 8 p.m. CT / 7 p.m. MT. — [SoS reminder PDF, via search](https://sos.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/news-releases/Reminders%20for%20voters%20ahead%20of%20Election%20Day_0.pdf)
- AP: "All Nebraska polls close at 9 p.m. ET." — [AP 2026 primary preview (News4Jax)](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/05/11/ap-decision-notes-what-to-expect-in-nebraskas-primaries/); [AP 2024 general preview (PBS)](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/what-to-expect-in-nebraska-on-election-day)

**Early (mail) voting:**
- All early ballots are due at the county election office by close of polls on Election Day. — [Nebraska Public Media / search summary](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/en/news/news-articles/dan-osborn-has-qualified-for-nebraska-senate-race/) **[snippet]**
- Headcount lists the return deadline as Nov 4. That conflicts with the above and is likely wrong. — [Headcount](https://www.headcount.org/state/nebraska/)
- In-person early voting runs Oct 5–Nov 2, 2026. The deadline to request a mail ballot is Oct 23. — [Fair Elections Center](https://fairelectionscenter.org/map/nebraska/)

**Early-ballot processing and counting (Neb. Rev. Stat. 32-1027; last amended by Laws 2026 LB1075 §32, operative July 18, 2026)** — [32-1027](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-1027):
- The counting board "may begin carrying out its duties not earlier than the second Friday before the election" (subsec. 1). For 2026 that is **Fri Oct 23**. Approved identification envelopes may be opened that day (subsec. 6).
- At the discretion of the election commissioner or county clerk, the board "may begin counting early ballots no earlier than twenty-four hours prior to the opening of the polls" (subsec. 6). That is about 8 a.m. CT **Mon Nov 2**.
- "No results shall be released prior to the closing of the polls on election day" (subsec. 8).
- The statute has no county-specific carve-out. Starting the count early is optional for each county.
- Amendment history: Laws 2020 LB1055, 2023 LB514, 2024 LB287, 2026 LB1075.

**LB1075 (2026)** — [Unicameral Update, Apr 10, 2026](https://update.legislature.ne.gov/?p=40949):
- This is the SoS-requested annual elections cleanup bill. It passed 49-0 and took effect immediately (operative July 18, 2026 per the statute page).
- It folded in LB884: ballots collected at midday must go directly to a "centralized location."
- It sets recount cost at $100 per precinct and bans petition circulation within 25 ft of a secure drop box.
- The article reports no change to count timing.

**All-mail precincts:**
- Allowed for candidate elections only in precincts of counties under 10,000 population, on application to the SoS. — [Civic Nebraska PDF citing a Nebraska Reform Institute presentation](https://civicnebraska.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/All-Mail-Elections-Nebraska-and-Nationally.pdf)
- A 2019-era legislative report said 67 counties were eligible and 11 ran all-mail in full. — [same](https://civicnebraska.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/All-Mail-Elections-Nebraska-and-Nationally.pdf)
- A 2017 KMTV report estimated about 1 in 22 voters (about 4.5%) live in all-mail precincts. — [3 News Now](https://www.3newsnow.com/news/investigations/for-some-nebraskans-voting-by-mail-is-the-default)

**Voter ID (LB514, 2023):**
- In person: photo ID is required. A voter without ID votes provisionally and must show ID to the county election office **by the Tuesday after the election** (Nov 10, 2026). — [SoS Voter ID brochure](https://sos.nebraska.gov/sites/sos.nebraska.gov/files/doc/Voter%20ID%20Brochure.pdf); [Common Cause NE](https://www.commoncause.org/nebraska/democracy-wire/faq-re-passed-nebraska-voter-id-bill/)
- Mail: the voter writes a DL/state ID number on the application, or encloses a copy of ID or a reasonable-impediment certification. — [same sources]
- Evnen called 2024 "the first general election with a voter ID requirement." — [Nebraska Examiner via search](https://nebraskaexaminer.com/?p=26379) **[snippet]**

**Canvass and certification:**
- The Board of State Canvassers meets "on the fourth Monday after each statewide primary and general election" and may adjourn day to day (32-1037). For 2026 that is **Mon Nov 30, 2026**. — [32-1037](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-1037)
- Check against 2024: the state board certified on Mon Dec 2, 2024, the fourth Monday after Nov 5. — [WOWT](https://www.wowt.com/2024/12/02/nebraska-officials-certify-statewide-2024-election-results/)
- Board members: Governor, SoS, AG, Treasurer, Auditor. — [Nebraska Public Media](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/en/news/news-articles/nebraska-board-of-canvassers-certifies-election-results/)
- County certificates of election are due "on or before the sixth Monday after the election" (32-1033). — [32-1033](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-1033)
- Example county timing: in one November election, Douglas County certified Nov 21. — [3 News Now](https://www.3newsnow.com/news/local-news/counting-continues-status-of-the-remaining-ballots-in-douglas-county) (undated; see section 2)

**Recount (32-1119):**
- A losing candidate is entitled to a recount when the margin is ≤1% of the votes received by the leading candidate (offices with more than 500 votes cast). The loser may waive it in writing.
- Statewide recounts occur "on the fifth Wednesday after the election" at 9 a.m. For 2026 that is **Wed Dec 2, 2026**.
- Source: [32-1119](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-1119)
- AP describes these recounts as automatic. It says the trigger is ≤1% "of the total vote", which differs slightly from the statute's "of the votes received by the candidate". AP may call a race subject to a recount if the lead is too large to overturn. — [AP primary preview](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/05/11/ap-decision-notes-what-to-expect-in-nebraskas-primaries/)

### Inferences
- A 1% margin in a two-way race at ~900k votes is about 4,500–9,000 votes, depending on which base is used. A recount would be expected only if the margin is under about 1 point.
- Because counties may tabulate early ballots from Monday morning, a large first dump of early ballots right at 9 p.m. ET is expected statewide.

### Gaps
- I did not read the statute that fixes the **county** canvass deadline. The sections checked (32-1032, 32-1033) do not state it. It sits in practice before the 4th-Monday state canvass.
- I did not get the full LB1075 bill text, so other 2026 changes to counting procedure cannot be ruled out.
- No current 2026 list of all-mail precincts was found.

## 2. Reporting units, official site, cadence, order of reporting, slow counties (Douglas, Lancaster, Sarpy)

### Takeaway
Reports come from 93 counties. More than three-quarters of them post all or nearly all early votes in their first update, often before any Election Day votes. Douglas (Omaha) and Lancaster (Lincoln) post few or no Election Day votes in their first update. Douglas holds late-arriving early ballots, including Election Day drop-box returns, plus provisionals and resolution ballots for days afterward. Those can run to 15,000–18,000 ballots.

### Cited Findings
**Statewide order (AP):**
- "Over three-quarters of the 93 counties usually report all or nearly all early and absentee results in their first update, often before any in-person Election Day results."
- "Nearly two-thirds of counties usually report few or no in-person Election Day results in their first update," Douglas and Lancaster among them.
- Source: [AP 2026 primary preview](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/05/11/ap-decision-notes-what-to-expect-in-nebraskas-primaries/)

**Douglas County first release:**
- The 8 p.m. CT first release is early ballots turned in through Monday afternoon (May 2024 primary). — [search summary of 3 News Now / WOWT coverage](https://www.wowt.com/2024/05/06/election-2024-douglas-county-officials-remind-early-voters-submit-ballots) **[snippet]**
- A search summary described a 2024-general release plan of 8:00, 8:45, 9:45, 10:45 and 11:45 p.m. CT, with early ballots first and then Election Day votes in time blocks. **I could not confirm this against a source page; treat it as unverified.**

**Douglas County post-election tail:**
- In one presidential general (article undated; "new machines" points to 2024 but this is unconfirmed), the early-ballot release was delayed and phased.
  - About 18,000 votes were left uncounted after election night.
  - That included 4,600 provisionals and 7,400 ballots dropped in drop boxes on Election Day.
  - Source: [3 News Now](https://www.3newsnow.com/news/investigations/high-turnout-delayed-early-ballot-reporting-in-douglas-county-election-commissioner-says)
- In another November election (undated, with a Legislature District 13 race):
  - Nearly 15,000 ballots remained: 6,765 early, 2,600 resolution and 5,560 provisional.
  - Most of the remaining early ballots were counted Friday, with results about 4–5 p.m.
  - Provisionals were counted Nov 18. The ID cure and early-ballot signature deadline was 5 p.m. Tuesday. County certification was Nov 21.
  - Source: [3 News Now](https://www.3newsnow.com/news/local-news/counting-continues-status-of-the-remaining-ballots-in-douglas-county)
- 2022 primary practice: late early ballots had signatures checked Wednesday, were opened Thursday and counted Friday. — [Nebraska Examiner, via search](https://nebraskaexaminer.com/briefs/douglas-county-adds-10600-last-minute-early-voting-ballots-to-nebraska-primary-totals/) **[snippet]**
- The same Examiner brief reported Douglas adding 10,600 last-minute early ballots to primary totals.

**Lancaster County** publishes timed election-night summary PDFs (e.g., "GN24 Election Summary Election Day 12:15AM"). — [Lancaster County](https://www.lancaster.ne.gov/DocumentCenter/View/25641/GN24-Election-Summary-Election-Day-1215AM) (403 on fetch; title only)

### Inferences
- Expect three phases:
  1. A 9:00–9:15 p.m. ET dump that is mostly early ballots statewide, including Omaha and Lincoln early ballots received through Monday.
  2. Rural Election Day precincts coming in through about 10 p.m.–1 a.m. ET.
  3. Douglas and Lancaster Election Day votes in batches until about midnight–1 a.m. CT. In Douglas, a residual of Election Day drop-box early ballots and provisionals is counted Friday and the following week.
- The post-election residual is concentrated in Douglas, which Osborn won. A narrow Ricketts lead of under about 1 point on election night could shrink in the days after. A narrow Osborn lead would probably hold or grow. The direction is inference, based on Osborn winning Douglas and early ballots leaning Osborn in Lancaster (section 4).

### Gaps
- The official results site (electionresults.nebraska.gov) was not fetched or verified. I found no update cadence for it.
- I found no 2026 release schedule for Douglas, Lancaster or Sarpy.
- No Sarpy-specific timing was found.
- Both 3 News Now articles are undated, so the year is not confirmed.

## 3. Historical timing: 2018–2024 (share counted by midnight, early-lead direction, shifts, AP call times)

### Takeaway
Nebraska counts fast. In 2020, about 71% of the vote was in by midnight ET. In 2024, AP called Fischer vs. Osborn (final margin R +6.67) at **12:06 a.m. ET**, about 3 hours after polls closed. The Republican lead **widened** through the night, from about 4 points at 11:30 p.m. to about 5 at midnight (both times as given by the Examiner) to 6.7 final, as rural votes came in. The close NE-02 House race took until Friday.

### Cited Findings
**2024 Senate (Fischer vs. Osborn):**
- Final result: Fischer 499,124 (53.19%), Osborn 436,493 (46.52%), write-ins 2,719. Margin 62,631 (6.67 pts). Total 938,336. — [Wikipedia 2024 NE Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Senate_election_in_Nebraska)
- AP called the race at **12:06 a.m. EST, Nov 6** (11:06 p.m. CT Nov 5). — [NOTUS](https://www.notus.org/2024-election/deb-fischer-dan-osborn-nebraska-senate-result); [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/11/06/g-s1-32826/nebraska-republican-deb-fischer-wins-reelection-campaign)
- The Examiner reported Fischer up nearly 4 pts at 11:30 p.m. and nearly 5 pts at midnight, "with more rural votes still coming in." The article's time zone is presumably CT, which would put both readings after the AP call; this is not stated. — [Nebraska Examiner, via search](https://nebraskaexaminer.com/?p=25674) **[snippet]**
- Osborn conceded Tuesday night at his watch party. — [Nebraska Public Media](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/es/news/news-articles/fischer-osborn-face-off-in-race-for-six-year-term-in-us-senate/)

**2024 NE-02 House:**
- AP called it for Bacon on **Fri Nov 8, 2024 at 6:11 p.m. ET** (about 5:11 p.m. CT), after an update put him up 7,150 votes. Vargas then conceded. — [Nebraska Examiner via search](https://nebraskaexaminer.com/?p=25829); [AP race call via KOB](https://kob.com/?p=718317)
- Harris won the NE-02 electoral vote "days before" the House call. — [WOWT](https://www.wowt.com/2024/11/06/election-night-2024-bacon-vargas-await-last-vote-counts) **[snippet]**

**2024 primary (May):**
- First AP results arrived at poll close. AP called the Senate primary for Ricketts at 9:10 p.m. ET.
- About 90% was counted by 12:10 a.m. ET; the final update came at 1:35 a.m. ET with about 98% counted.
- Source: [AP 2026 primary preview](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/05/11/ap-decision-notes-what-to-expect-in-nebraskas-primaries/)

**2026 primary (May 12):** AP called Ricketts about 20 minutes after poll close. Ricketts won 78% of early ballots cast in the primary. — [Nebraska Public Media](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/en/news/news-articles/2026-us-senate-primary-results/) **[snippet]**

**2020 general:**
- First votes were reported at 9:02 p.m. ET. About 71% of the total vote was reported by midnight ET.
- AP called the statewide presidential race at 9:00 p.m. ET (Trump 59%, Biden 39%).
- Source: [AP via PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/what-to-expect-in-nebraska-on-election-day)

**Early-vote share of the total:** about 56% in 2020 and about 39% in 2022. — [AP via PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/what-to-expect-in-nebraska-on-election-day)

**Certification and recount, 2024:** The state board certified Dec 2, 2024. The one recount was a Dawson Public Power District seat with a 20-vote gap. — [Nebraska Public Media](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/en/news/news-articles/nebraska-board-of-canvassers-certifies-election-results/)

### Inferences
- 2024 pattern: a moderate first dump, then a lead for the Republican that widened as rural Election Day votes came in. That is a "red shift" after the early dump, though the dump itself was not Osborn-led statewide as far as found.
- With 2026 polls roughly tied, AP probably cannot call the race on election night if the margin is under about 2–3 pts. Douglas's residual (about 15–18k ballots in past generals) is counted Friday and the next week, and provisional cures close Tue Nov 10.
- A margin of 1% or less triggers a mandatory recount on Dec 2, 2026, after the Nov 30 canvass.

### Gaps
- **2018 Senate (Fischer–Raybould) and Governor (Ricketts–Krist):** no AP call times or count curves found.
- **2022 Governor (Pillen–Blood) general:** no call time found. Only the 2022 primary was found (Pillen GOP primary called about 10:30 p.m. local, per [WOWT](https://www.wowt.com/2022/05/10/election-2022-all-eyes-republican-race-nebraska-governor/)).
- I found no share-counted-by-midnight figure for 2018, 2022 or 2024 generals, and no first-dump margin for 2024 Senate.
- The exact date and time of the Harris NE-02 call were not found.

## 4. Partisan lean of early (mail) vs. Election Day votes; Osborn's pattern by county type

### Takeaway
In Lancaster in 2024, early ballots were much more pro-Osborn (about 65%) than Election Day ballots (about 54%). Osborn won only four counties: Douglas, Lancaster, Sarpy and Thurston. Statewide, early votes lean Democratic/Osborn in the metros, while rural counties dump early and Election Day ballots together and lean heavily Republican.

### Cited Findings
**Lancaster County 2024 official final:**

| Ballot type | Osborn | Fischer | Osborn share / margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total | 91,356 | 65,295 | — |
| Early/absentee | 39,415 | 21,606 | about 64.6% / +17.8k |
| Election Day | 50,022 | 42,709 | about 53.9% / +7.3k |
| Provisional | 1,919 | 980 | — |
| Early received through Nov 2 (pre-election release) | 35,226 | 19,440 | — |

Sources: [Lancaster County docs, via search](https://www.lancaster.ne.gov/DocumentCenter/View/25877) **[snippet; arithmetic by search tool, not re-verified]**

**County pattern:** Osborn won four counties: Thurston and Sarpy (both Trump) and Douglas and Lancaster (both Harris). Fischer won the other 89 and did worse in the east and along the I-80 corridor. — [Nebraska Public Media county analysis](https://nebraskapublicmedia.org/en/news/news-articles/nebraskans-voted-along-long-held-political-divides-county-analysis-shows/)

**Primary early-vote lean:** In the 2022 and 2024 primaries, about 39% of GOP and about 65% of Democratic primary votes were cast early. — [AP 2026 primary preview](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/05/11/ap-decision-notes-what-to-expect-in-nebraskas-primaries/)

### Inferences
- The 9 p.m. ET first dump will include Douglas and Lancaster early ballots, which lean strongly Osborn, alongside rural counties' early ballots. Osborn's share is likely highest early, with the margin moving toward Ricketts as rural and metro Election Day votes arrive.
- 2024 showed exactly this widening. A modest Osborn lead in the first dump should not be read as decisive.

### Gaps
- No Douglas or Sarpy early vs. Election Day split was found for 2024.
- No 2026 party breakdown of early-ballot returns was found. Nebraska's registration is about 49% R, 27% D and 22% nonpartisan as of Oct 2024, per [AP/PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/what-to-expect-in-nebraska-on-election-day).
