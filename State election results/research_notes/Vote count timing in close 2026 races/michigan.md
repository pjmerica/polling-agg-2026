## 2. 2026 rules: poll closing, deadlines, pre-processing, ban on early results

### Takeaway
- **Poll closing:** 8 p.m. ET for most of the state. Four western Upper Peninsula counties close at 9 p.m. ET.
- **Early results are banned.** No results may be generated or reported before 8 p.m. ET, and leaking them is a felony.
- **Early tabulation:** Cities and townships with 5,000+ residents (or county boards) may pre-process and tabulate absentee ballots on any of the 8 days before Election Day. Any jurisdiction may do so on the Monday before.
- **Absentee deadline:** 8 p.m. on Election Day.
- **Military/overseas grace:** Ballots postmarked by Election Day count if received within 6 days.
- **Provisional ballots:** Clerks must decide within 6 days whether each one counts.

### Cited Findings
- **Poll closing.** Polls close at 8 p.m. local time. The Central-time western UP counties close at 9 p.m. ET — [AP Decision Notes](https://www.13abc.com/2026/08/03/ap-decision-notes-what-expect-michigans-state-primary/). The four counties are Gogebic, Iron, Dickinson and Menominee — [WDIV/ClickOnDetroit, Oct 14, 2026](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2026/10/14/why-arent-michigan-election-results-instant-how-our-votes-are-counted/).
- **Early processing (MCL 168.765a(11)).**
  - Who: a city or township with at least 5,000 residents, or a county board of election commissioners (per §764d).
  - When: any of the 8 days before Election Day, from the second Monday before through the Monday before. Any city or township, regardless of size, may process on the Monday before.
  - Hours: 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  - On Election Day itself, processing starts no earlier than 7 a.m. (subsection 6).
  - Source: [MCL 168.765a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-765a).
- **Notice requirements (subsection 12).**
  - The clerk notifies the Secretary of State at least 28 days before the election.
  - The Secretary of State publishes the list of participating jurisdictions at least 20 days before.
  - The clerk posts a public notice at least 18 days before. Revisions are due by the 11th day before.
  - Source: [MCL 168.765a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-765a).
  - The official 2026 calendar lists Fri Oct 23, 2026 as the deadline for clerks to post revised notices — [MI SoS Election Dates PDF](https://www.michigan.gov/sos/-/media/Project/Websites/sos/Election-Administrators/Election-Dates.pdf).
- **Ban on early results.** Subsection (13): "Election results must not be generated, printed, or reported before 8 p.m. on election day." Subsection (9): anyone who discloses a result before polls can legally close commits a felony. Source: [MCL 168.765a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-765a).
  - WDIV adds that pre-processing does not let results out early. Early and absentee data are uploaded when polls close, so several data streams can arrive at once — [ClickOnDetroit](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2026/10/14/why-arent-michigan-election-results-instant-how-our-votes-are-counted/).
- **Daily counting-board procedure (subsection 15).** At the end of each day, tabulated ballots are sealed, the poll book is signed by inspectors from both major parties, and the daily count is posted. Each Election Day precinct has a matching absent voter counting board. At least one inspector from each major party must be present — [MCL 168.765a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-765a).
- **Interaction with early voting (subsection 16).**
  - A ballot received on an early voting day goes to the counting board the next day.
  - It is processed only after the early voter history is recorded in the qualified voter file.
  - An absentee ballot is cancelled if the voter also voted at an early voting site.
  - Source: [MCL 168.765a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-765a).
- **Early in-person voting.** At least 9 days are guaranteed — [ClickOnDetroit](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2026/10/14/why-arent-michigan-election-results-instant-how-our-votes-are-counted/). The 2026 constitutional early voting period is Oct 24 to Nov 1 — [MI SoS / search summary](https://www.michigan.gov/sos/elections/voting/early-in-person-voting); [Fox 2](https://www.fox2detroit.com/news/election-2026-michigan-early-voting-in-person-begins-this-weekend). Voters can feed absentee ballots into a tabulator during early voting — [WZZM](https://www.wzzm13.com/article/news/politics/elections/michigan-voting-early-in-person-absentee-polling-times/69-06043520-abe5-423a-b4e0-86bd0f97255c).
- **Absentee receipt deadline.** The clerk must receive the ballot by 8 p.m. on Election Day — [WZZM](https://www.wzzm13.com/article/news/politics/elections/michigan-voting-early-in-person-absentee-polling-times/69-06043520-abe5-423a-b4e0-86bd0f97255c).
- **Military/overseas grace period.** Proposal 2 (2022) makes these ballots timely if postmarked by Election Day and received within 6 days. SB 259, signed by Whitmer, put this into statute — [Fox 2](https://fox2detroit.com/news/absentee-voting-expanded-in-michigan-for-military-service-members-overseas); [MSU Extension on Prop 22-2](https://www.canr.msu.edu/resources/statewide-ballot-proposal-22-2-promote-the-vote-2022).
- **Provisional ballots (MCL 168.813).**
  - Within 6 days after the election, the city or township clerk decides whether each provisional ballot is eligible and should be tabulated.
  - A ballot counts if a valid registration is found, or if identity and residence are established with ID plus a residence document.
  - Results go to the county canvassers within 7 days, sooner if practicable.
  - Source: [MCL 168.813](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/documents/mcl/pdf/mcl-168-813-amended.pdf) (via search excerpt).

### Inferences
- Large Democratic-leaning cities such as Detroit, Ann Arbor, Lansing and Grand Rapids pass the 5,000-resident threshold. So most absentee ballots in big jurisdictions should already be tabulated before 8 p.m. and can post in the first hour or two after polls close, subject to each county's upload practice. That differs sharply from 2020, when Michigan law allowed almost no pre-processing.
- The 6-day military window and the provisional ballots are tiny in volume. They matter only if the margin is within a few thousand votes.

### Gaps
- I did not pull the 2023 Public Act numbers (commonly cited as PA 81/82 of 2023) from a primary source. The rules above are quoted from the current text of MCL 168.765a instead.
- I did not find the Secretary of State's published list of 2026 early-tabulating jurisdictions (due about Oct 14).
- **Unverified ID-cure rule.** Under the 2022 constitution, a voter without ID signs an affidavit and votes a regular ballot. The "6-day cure" for provisional ballots cast by voters lacking ID came from the task brief. I could not confirm it beyond the 6-day clerk determination in §813.

## 3. Canvass, certification, recount

### Takeaway
- County canvassers must finish within 14 days.
- If a county board fails to certify, the Board of State Canvassers certifies by the 20th day.
- Michigan's automatic statewide recount trigger is now 0.1% of total votes cast, not 2,000 votes (2024 PA 74, effective Apr 2, 2025).

### Cited Findings
- **County canvass (MCL 168.822(1)).** The county board must complete the canvass "no later than the fourteenth day after the election." If it fails, records go immediately to the Board of State Canvassers, which certifies no later than the 20th day. The section was amended by 2023 PA 269 and 2024 PA 74 — [MCL 168.822](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-822).
- **Automatic recount (MCL 168.880a(1)).** A recount of all precincts must be conducted when a statewide election is certified "as having been determined by a vote differential of 0.1% or less of the total number of votes cast." The section was amended by 2024 PA 74, effective April 2, 2025 — [MCL 168.880a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-880a).
  - The old 2,000-vote figure appears in older sources — [Verified Voting](https://verifiedvoting.org/recountlaw/michigan/).
  - AP's 2026 primary preview also states the 0.1% rule — [AP Decision Notes](https://www.13abc.com/2026/08/03/ap-decision-notes-what-expect-michigans-state-primary/).
- **Requested recounts.** Under the 2024 recount law, a candidate may request a recount only with a "good-faith belief" and a "reasonable chance" of winning absent counting errors — [AP Decision Notes](https://www.13abc.com/2026/08/03/ap-decision-notes-what-expect-michigans-state-primary/).
- **AP calls under recount.** AP may call a race that faces a recount if the lead is too large for a recount to change the outcome — [AP Decision Notes](https://www.13abc.com/2026/08/03/ap-decision-notes-what-expect-michigans-state-primary/).

### Inferences
- At about 4.5 million votes (2022/2018-scale midterm turnout), 0.1% is about 4,500 votes, larger than the old 2,000. Under 2024 turnout (about 5.7M) it would be about 5,700. Turnout figures are approximate and not sourced here.
- Expect county certification by about Nov 17, 2026 (day 14). State certification would come later in November. The exact §842 deadline for the Board of State Canvassers was not pulled.

### Gaps
- MCL 168.842 (state board deadline) was not fetched. The "~day 20" figure is confirmed only for the case where a county fails to certify.

## 4. Reporting units, results sites, slow jurisdictions, reporting order

### Takeaway
- Michigan reports from 83 counties.
- The big Detroit-area counties and Kent/Genesee/Washtenaw report little in their first updates.
- Wayne County (Detroit) absentees have repeatedly been the last big Democratic batch, most recently in the Aug 2026 primary.

### Cited Findings
- **Early updates.** First statewide updates typically mix absentee, early in-person and Election Day votes. Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, Kent, Genesee and Washtenaw "tend to release relatively few results of any vote type in their first updates." Counties vary in when and how they release results — [AP Decision Notes, Aug 2026](https://www.13abc.com/2026/08/03/ap-decision-notes-what-expect-michigans-state-primary/).
- **Pace in the Aug 2024 Democratic Senate primary.**
  - First results at 8:03 p.m. ET.
  - Past 50% counted at 12:03 a.m.
  - About 95% counted by 4:12 a.m.
  - Source: [AP Decision Notes](https://www.13abc.com/2026/08/03/ap-decision-notes-what-expect-michigans-state-primary/).
- **Aug 2026 primary.** Wayne County absentee ballots were still being counted overnight. The count was at 96% by 8:49 a.m. Wednesday — [Fox 4](https://www.fox4news.com/news/abdul-el-sayed-projected-defeat-haley-stevens-democratic-senate-primary-race.amp).
- **Precinct balancing.** Bipartisan pairs of workers must balance totals precinct by precinct before tallies go to county canvassers. This covers all 83 counties — [ClickOnDetroit](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2026/10/14/why-arent-michigan-election-results-instant-how-our-votes-are-counted/).
- **State results site.** The state posts unofficial results at the Secretary of State's results site (mvic.sos.state.mi.us). For the Aug 2026 primary it showed 83/83 counties as unofficial — [MI SoS results page](https://mvic.sos.state.mi.us/votehistory/Index?type=C&amp=&electionDate=8-4-2026).
- **Detroit in 2024.** Detroit expected faster 2024 results thanks to the 8-day pre-tabulation and early voting. One adviser said results could be in by midnight — [Bridge Michigan](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/detroit-predicting-high-turnout-faster-count-presidential-race/).

### Inferences
- **Likely order on Nov 3:**
  1. 8 to 9 p.m. ET: mixed first drops from smaller and rural counties, which lean Republican, plus some pre-tabulated absentees.
  2. Later: Oakland, Macomb, Kent and Washtenaw fill in through late evening.
  3. Last: Wayne/Detroit absentee and Election Day totals arrive after midnight, and can finish the next morning.
- This implies an early Rogers lead that narrows or flips overnight, the same pattern as 2020 and 2024 Senate and the 2026 primary.

### Gaps
- No source gave the exact number of reporting jurisdictions (the ~1,500 cities/townships figure is unverified here). No source described the update cadence of the state site.
- I found no 2026-general-specific guidance from Detroit's clerk or Wayne County.

## 5. Law changes since 2022 affecting 2026

### Takeaway
The main changes since 2022:
- Proposal 2 (2022): 9+ days of early voting, the 6-day military window, and the affidavit option for voters without ID.
- 2023 statutes: up to 8 days of absentee pre-tabulation in jurisdictions with 5,000+ residents, and the ban on reporting before 8 p.m.
- 2024 PA 74 (effective 2025): automatic recount trigger changed to 0.1%, a "reasonable chance" standard for requested recounts, and amendments to the canvass section.

### Cited Findings
- **Early voting:** at least 9 days of early in-person voting — [ClickOnDetroit](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2026/10/14/why-arent-michigan-election-results-instant-how-our-votes-are-counted/).
- **Military/overseas grace:** 6-day window — [Fox 2](https://fox2detroit.com/news/absentee-voting-expanded-in-michigan-for-military-service-members-overseas).
- **Pre-tabulation:** 8-day pre-tabulation rules — [MCL 168.765a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-765a).
- **Recount and canvass:** the 0.1% recount trigger, and amendments to the canvass sections by 2023 PA 269 and 2024 PA 74 — [MCL 168.880a](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-880a); [MCL 168.822](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-168-822); [AP Decision Notes](https://www.13abc.com/2026/08/03/ap-decision-notes-what-expect-michigans-state-primary/).

### Inferences
- 2026 is the first Michigan midterm general held with all of these rules. 2024 is the only general election run under the pre-tabulation and early voting rules.

### Gaps
- I did not confirm the 2023 Public Act numbers from a primary source.

## 6. History, 2018 to 2024: early lead, shift and AP call times

### Takeaway
- In every close Michigan statewide race since 2018, the Republican led early and Democrats gained as Wayne/Detroit and other big counties reported.
- Close races (2020 Senate, 2024 Senate, the 2026 primary) were called between about 14 and 25 hours after polls closed.
- Comfortable races (2018 Senate and Governor, 2022 Governor) were called on election night or by about 1:30 a.m.

### Cited Findings

**2018 Senate (Stabenow D vs. James R)**
- AP called it just after 11 p.m. on election night — [WSBT](https://wsbt.com/topic/Pete%20Hoekstra).
- At 11:09 p.m., with 49% tallied, Stabenow led 52.2 to 45.8 (NYT figures) — [Metro Times archive](https://www.metrotimes.com/issue-archives/oct-31-nov-6-2018/).
- Search summaries also said the candidates were within a few hundred votes for much of the night. Treat this as unverified, since it seems inconsistent with the 11:09 p.m. snapshot.

**2018 Governor (Whitmer)**
- AP called it with about 22% of the vote counted, on election night. I did not find the exact time — [Metro Times](https://www.metrotimes.com/issue-archives/oct-31-nov-6-2018/).

**2020 Senate (Peters D vs. James R)**
- James led through much of Tuesday and Wednesday. Absentee votes from the big cities, with Wayne and Kalamazoo still counting, put Peters ahead.
- AP called it about 24 hours after polls closed, "after 9 p.m." Wednesday, Nov 4 — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2020/11/07/932422850/gop-senate-candidate-john-james-refuses-to-concede-claims-irregularities-in-vote); [Detour Detroiter](https://detourdetroiter.com/gary-peters-wins-michigan-senate-race).
- At 98% reporting, Peters led by about 60,000 votes, 49.6% to 48.5% — [Detour Detroiter](https://detourdetroiter.com/gary-peters-wins-michigan-senate-race).

**2020 President**
- Biden won Michigan by about 154,000 votes out of more than 5.5 million — [AP via ClickOnDetroit](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/politics/2024/11/06/why-ap-called-michigan-for-trump/).
- AP's call time for 2020 president was not retrieved.

**2022 Governor (Whitmer vs. Dixon)**
- AP called it at 1:21 a.m. Wednesday, with most large population centers reporting. Fox projected it late Tuesday.
- At 6 a.m., with more than 85% counted, Whitmer led 53 to 46 — [Bridge Michigan](https://www.bridgemi.com/michigan-government/gretchen-whitmer-leads-tudor-dixon-race-michigan-governor); [Scripps/WPTV](https://www.wptv.com/news/national/michigan-gov-gretchen-whitmer-wins-reelection-tudor-dixon-concedes).

**2024 Senate (Slotkin D vs. Rogers R)**
- Rogers "jumped out to a significant early lead" that lasted into early Wednesday.
- Slotkin gained as Wayne County and other big Democratic counties reported, and her lead grew with most later updates.
- AP called it at 3:12 p.m. ET Wednesday, Nov 6, with Slotkin ahead by about 18,700 votes. Rogers would have needed more than 64% of the remaining vote.
- Source: [AP, "Why AP called the Michigan Senate race" (via KPRC)](https://www.click2houston.com/news/politics/2024/11/06/why-ap-called-the-michigan-senate-race-for-elissa-slotkin/).

**2024 President**
- AP called Michigan for Trump at 12:54 p.m. ET Wednesday, Nov 6. Harris could not have caught up even winning 9 of every 10 remaining ballots.
- Trump improved in Wayne County compared with 2020 — [AP via ClickOnDetroit](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/politics/2024/11/06/why-ap-called-michigan-for-trump/).
- Unofficial count at the call: Trump 2,793,837, Harris 2,712,623 — [WTOL](https://wtol.com/article/news/politics/elections/ap-trump-wins-michigan-election-results-2024/512-6a2c2dc0-898d-4a10-a0a8-c6660999aaf7).

**Aug 2026 Democratic Senate primary**
- AP called it just before 10 a.m. the next day — see Section 1.

### Inferences
- If the Nov 2026 Senate margin is about 2 points or less, expect no AP call on election night. The call would likely come Wednesday late morning to afternoon, matching 2024 Senate (about 19 hours after polls closed) and the 2026 primary (about 14 hours), and not 2020 (about 25 hours). 2020 was slower because pre-processing was not allowed then.
- A margin of 5 points or more is likely callable between about 10 p.m. and 1:30 a.m., matching 2018 and 2022.
- Whoever leads first, expect the D share to rise after midnight.

### Gaps

- I did not find the magnitude of the overnight shift in percentage points for 2020 or 2024 Senate.
- Not retrieved: the call time for 2020 president, the exact 2018 Governor call time, the 2022 Proposal 3 call, and the final 2024 Senate margin.

## 7. Partisan lean by vote method (2022/2024)

### Takeaway
In 2024 the statewide split was:
- 39.2% absentee
- 21.0% early in-person
- 39.4% Election Day

Early in-person and Election Day voting were heaviest in Trump-leaning counties, which implies absentees lean Democratic. I found no Harris/Trump split by method.

### Cited Findings
- Election Day was the most common method in 66 of 83 counties — [Bridge Michigan](https://www.bridgemi.com/michigan-government/most-michiganders-voted-early-or-absentee-2024-see-how-your-county-cast-ballots).
- Barry, Allegan and Kalkaska (strongly Trump) cast more than a third of their ballots during early in-person voting. Trump won all 25 counties where at least half of voters voted on Election Day — [Bridge Michigan](https://www.bridgemi.com/michigan-government/most-michiganders-voted-early-or-absentee-2024-see-how-your-county-cast-ballots).
- Nearly two-thirds of 2024 Michigan voters voted early or by mail — [ClickOnDetroit](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2026/10/14/why-arent-michigan-election-results-instant-how-our-votes-are-counted/).
- About 63% of the Aug 2024 primary vote was early or by mail — [AP Decision Notes](https://www.13abc.com/2026/08/03/ap-decision-notes-what-expect-michigans-state-primary/).
- More than 1 million ballots had been cast by the Friday before the Aug 2026 primary — [AP Decision Notes](https://www.13abc.com/2026/08/03/ap-decision-notes-what-expect-michigans-state-primary/).

### Inferences
- In Michigan, early in-person voting is not the Democratic-leaning stream it is in some states. Absentee ballots are the Democratic-leaning stream, especially Detroit's, which are reported late.

### Gaps
- I found no 2022 method split and no candidate split by vote type from an official or exit-poll source.
