## 1. 2026 ballot, majority rule and runoff date

### Takeaway
The governor general election is a two-candidate race: Bottoms (D) vs. Jackson (R). The Libertarian, Chase Oliver, is listed as withdrawn. With no third candidate, a governor runoff could only come from write-ins pushing both majors below 50%+1, which is practically nil. ## 2. Statutory timing rules for 2026 (processing, tabulation, 8 p.m. reporting, deadlines)

### Takeaway
Under SB 189 (2024), now codified, **every absentee ballot accepted by the Monday before Election Day and every advance (early in-person) vote must be tabulated and reported by 8:00 p.m. ET on Election Day, or within one hour of the last poll closing in the county, whichever is later.** Polls close at 7 p.m. ET. Absentee ballots may be opened and scanned from 8 a.m. on the third Monday before the election (Oct 12, 2026). Tabulation may start at 7 a.m. on Election Day, but no results may be disclosed before 7 p.m. Mail ballots must arrive by poll close; military and overseas (UOCAVA) ballots get 3 extra days.

### Cited Findings
- **SB 189, Section 8, amending O.C.G.A. § 21-2-386(a)(3) (effective July 1, 2024).** The bill text reads: "all absentee ballots that have been verified and accepted ... by the Monday prior to the day of the primary, election, or runoff shall be tabulated and the results reported by no later than 8:00 P.M. on the day of such primary, election, or runoff or within one hour of the closing of all polls in such county, whichever occurs later." Section 9 (effective Jan 1, 2025) re-enacts the same language in a rewrite of § 21-2-386. — [SB 189 enrolled text (24 LC 47 3110S), gov.georgia.gov](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2024-signed-legislation/sb-189/download)
- **SB 189, Section 10, amending O.C.G.A. § 21-2-493(a).** The bill text reads: "such votes cast by advance voting shall be tabulated and the results reported by no later than 8:00 P.M. on such day or within one hour of the closing of all polls in such county, whichever occurs later." After that come Election Day ballots, then "provisional ballots ... and those absentee ballots as provided in subparagraph (a)(1)(G) [UOCAVA] ... from day to day until completed." — [SB 189 text](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2024-signed-legislation/sb-189/download)
- **SB 189 effective dates (Section 13).** Sections 8 and 10 took effect July 1, 2024. Sections 4, 6 and 9 took effect Jan 1, 2025. Section 7 (the official tally must be read from the human-readable text, not the QR code) was set for July 1, 2026. All the reporting provisions are **in force for Nov 2026**. — [SB 189 text](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2024-signed-legislation/sb-189/download)
- **The QR-code deadline was pushed back.**
  - A June 2026 special-session law delays QR-code removal to January 2028 (Fox5) or 2028 (Brennan). It also creates an Election Equipment Specifications and Standards Committee, which reports by Jan 31, 2027.
  - Voters in 2026 use the same Dominion ballot-marking devices.
  - Sources: [Fox 5 Atlanta, Jun 25 2026](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/georgia-election-bill-deadlines-new-law-delays-ballot-qr-code-removal); [Brennan Center, Oct 14 2026](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/analysis-opinion/how-georgias-voters-will-cast-their-ballots-2026); [Georgia Recorder, Jun 20 2026](https://georgiarecorder.com/2026/06/20/bill-extending-georgias-ballot-qr-code-deadline-clears-senate-in-a-saturday-vote/)
- **Absentee scanning start, § 21-2-386(a)(2)(A).** The statute reads: "Beginning at 8:00 A.M. on the third Monday prior to the day of the primary, election, or runoff". For Nov 3 that is Oct 12, 2026. Seven days' written notice to the SoS is required. — [FindLaw § 21-2-386](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-21-elections/ga-code-sect-21-2-386/)
  - Fulton County announced it will begin processing and scanning on **Mon Oct 26, 2026, 8 a.m.** — [Fulton County Registration & Elections (via search)](https://www.fultoncountyga.gov/Inside-Fulton-County/Fulton-County-Departments/Registration-and-Elections)
- **Tabulation start, § 21-2-386(a)(3).** Tabulation may begin "after 7:00 A.M." on Election Day, and no balloting information may be disclosed "before 7:00 P.M. on election day". — [FindLaw § 21-2-386](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-21-elections/ga-code-sect-21-2-386/)
  - Example: Athens-Clarke will process absentees on Oct 27–29 and Nov 2. On Nov 3 it begins early tabulation of absentees at 4:00 p.m. and of advance votes at 5:00 p.m. — [ACCGov notice](https://www.accgov.com/m/newsflash/home/detail/6999)
- **Fallback reporting rule, § 21-2-386(d).** Absentee returns are to be reported "as soon as possible following the closing of the polls". If they are not reported by 5:00 p.m. the day after the election, the State Election Board may convene a performance review board. — [FindLaw § 21-2-386](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-21-elections/ga-code-sect-21-2-386/)
- **Mail-ballot receipt deadline.** Mail ballots "must be received by your county registrar by 7 p.m. on Election Day". — [georgia.gov](https://georgia.gov/node/34081)
  - Ballots returned after polls close are not counted (§ 21-2-386(a)(1)(F)).
  - Military and overseas ballots postmarked by Election Day count if "received within the three-day period" after it (§ 21-2-386(a)(1)(G)), so by **Fri Nov 6, 2026**. — [FindLaw](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-21-elections/ga-code-sect-21-2-386/)
- **Cure deadline.** A voter whose absentee ballot was rejected has "until the end of the period for verifying provisional ballots" (§ 21-2-419(c)) to cure it. — [FindLaw § 21-2-386(a)(1)(C)](https://codes.findlaw.com/ga/title-21-elections/ga-code-sect-21-2-386/)
- **Provisional ballots.** These are counted once the issue is resolved with the County Board of Registrars. — [georgia.gov 2026 general election page (search summary)](https://georgia.gov/georgia-general-election-2026)
- **2026 calendar** (Protect Democracy Georgia 2026 handout, extracted locally):
  - Early voting: Oct 13–Oct 30
  - Mail-ballot request deadline: Oct 23
  - Election Day: Nov 3
  - County certification deadline: Nov 9
  - State certification: Nov 20
  - Deadline to contest: Nov 25
  - Statutes cited: § 21-2-493(k), § 21-2-495(c), §§ 21-2-499(b) and 21-2-502.
  - Source: [Protect Democracy GA handout](https://protectdemocracy.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/GA-Handout.pdf)
  - Early voting ending Oct 30 is confirmed by [Fox 5, Oct 20 2026](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/georgia-2026-elections-more-than-1m-georgians-have-voted-early)
- **Certification.** Counties must certify by 5 p.m. Monday Nov 9, 2026. The SoS certifies statewide races by 5 p.m. on the 17th day, Nov 20, 2026. — [Brennan Center certification guide](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/georgia-election-certification-processes-and-guardrails); [Worth County notice](https://www.worthcountyboc.com/elections/page/official-notice-voters)
  - A Brennan PDF also lists Dec 7 and Dec 18. These match the **runoff** schedule (Monday after Dec 1, and Dec 1 + 17 days). That is my inference.

### Inferences
- **8 p.m. on the SoS/Enhanced Voting site.** Expect a large batch of early and absentee results between 7 and 8 p.m. ET. In 2024 the SoS expected about 70–75% of all votes by 8 p.m. In 2026 about 1.06M had voted by Oct 21 with 9 days of early voting left. The early and mail share will likely again be well over half of the total vote. That is an inference from turnout pace, since 2022 total turnout was about 4M.
- **"Within one hour of the closing of all polls in such county".** A court-ordered extension at a precinct (common in Fulton/DeKalb in past cycles; not sourced here for 2026) legally delays that county's 8 p.m. early-vote deadline. Any statewide result can also not be disclosed until all Georgia polls close.
- **Ballots counted after election night are a small pool:**
  - mail ballots that arrive on Election Day itself (not covered by the 8 p.m. rule, which applies only to those accepted by Monday)
  - provisionals
  - UOCAVA ballots through Nov 6
  - cured ballots
  - Historically this pool is tens of thousands at most. That is an inference: 2020 had about 14k left by Nov 5 night ([CBS 2020](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/live-updates/georgia-2020-election-results)).

## 3. State Election Board rules and law changes since 2022 that affect 2026

### Takeaway
SB 202's processing timeline (from 2021) and SB 189's 8 p.m. reporting mandate (from 2024) are the main speed drivers. The SEB's fall-2024 rules were blocked or voided: the hand count of ballots at precincts and "reasonable inquiry" before certification. Courts held that certification is mandatory. A June 2026 law delays the QR-code ban to 2028 and makes hand recounts automatic for governor and lieutenant governor races at ≤0.5%.

### Cited Findings
- In October 2024, Fulton Superior Judge McBurney ruled that county board members must certify results even when they raise concerns. He blocked the SEB hand-count rule as "too much, too late". — [NPR, Oct 15 2024](https://www.npr.org/2024/10/15/nx-s1-5144185/georgia-election-certification-rules)
- Judge Thomas Cox Jr. declared the certification and hand-count rules, among others, "illegal, unconstitutional and void". The Georgia Supreme Court declined to reinstate the seven rules before the 2024 election. — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/10/15/nx-s1-5144185/georgia-election-certification-rules); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/georgia-supreme-court-wont-reinstate-controversial-rules-before-election-day)
- **2026 hand-recount change.** "When one of the highest state offices is at the top of the ballot, such as Governor or Lieutenant Governor, and the race finishes within one-half of one percent, the Secretary of State must order a full hand recount of every ballot cast in that race, in every county." The SoS directs it within 2 business days after the county certification deadline. Other races, including US Senate, still need a written request from the losing candidate within 2 business days after county certification. — [Cobb Courier, Oct 21 2026](https://cobbcountycourier.com/2026/10/which-comes-first-after-election-day-certification-or-a-recount/); [Fox 5, Jun 25 2026](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/georgia-election-bill-deadlines-new-law-delays-ballot-qr-code-removal)
  - The Current reported this differently: "hand recounts on the two top-ticket races in every election". — [The Current, Jun 20 2026](https://thecurrentga.org/2026/06/20/bill-extending-georgias-ballot-qr-code-deadline-clears-senate-in-a-saturday-vote/)
- Risk-limiting audits after the 2026 election cover US Senate, US House, Governor, Lt. Gov, SoS and AG. One more statewide office is chosen on the Thursday after Election Day. — [Brennan Center, Oct 14 2026](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/analysis-opinion/how-georgias-voters-will-cast-their-ballots-2026)
- The general recount threshold is a margin of 0.5% or less (§ 21-2-495(c)). — [Protect Democracy GA handout](https://protectdemocracy.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/GA-Handout.pdf)
- SB 189 also delays voter challenges filed within 45 days of an election until after it. — [GSU Law Review](https://www.gsulawreview.org/latest/sb-189-elections-and-primaries/)

### Inferences
- **Hand recount if the governor margin is ≤0.5%.** At ~4M votes, 0.5% is roughly 20k votes. The hand recount would start around Nov 11–12 and must finish before the Nov 20 state certification. In 2020 a statewide hand audit of ~5M ballots took Nov 13–19 ([Moultrie Observer, Nov 19 2020](https://moultrieobserver.com/2020/11/19/update-unprecedented-statewide-audit-reaffirms-biden-victory-in-georgia)).
- **AP behavior in that case.** AP declined to call Georgia 2020 (0.24% margin) until the hand audit finished. In a sub-0.5% governor race, markets should expect **no AP call until ~Nov 18–20**.

### Gaps
- Bill number of the June 2026 special-session law not found. The exact statutory wording of the automatic recount trigger (mandatory vs. on request; "top-ticket" scope) has **conflicting summaries**. Read the enrolled act.
- The final appellate outcome of the 2024 SEB rule cases was not found. A 2025 Georgia Supreme Court merits ruling, if any, is not verified.

## 4. Reporting units, results site, cadence and slow counties

### Takeaway
Results come in from 159 counties through the SoS's Enhanced Voting site (app.enhancedvoting.com, linked from results.sos.ga.gov). It replaced Clarity. Expect a large early/absentee batch around 7:30–8 p.m. ET, Election Day precincts through about 10:30 p.m.–1 a.m., and metro Atlanta counties last. Fulton and other metro counties have a history of late-night batches and reporting glitches. Chatham in 2024 showed "all precincts reporting" without early votes.

### Cited Findings
- The official 2026 primary results ran on Enhanced Voting: app.enhancedvoting.com/results/public/Georgia/elections/GeneralPrimary51926. — [Enhanced Voting GA results](https://app.enhancedvoting.com/results/public/Georgia/elections/GeneralPrimary51926)
- Raffensperger, 2024: "That's 70% that you'll know by 8 p.m. and our goal is for every county to have all the results reported by the end of the night." Smaller counties aimed to finish by 10:30 p.m. — [Atlanta News First, Oct 23 2024](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2024/10/23/raffensperger-39-million-georgians-expected-cast-ballots-before-early-voting-ends); [WCTV/ANF, Nov 1 2024](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2024/11/01/georgia-record-early-voting-could-mean-earlier-results-election-night/)
- **Chatham, 2024.** The SoS site showed Chatham with all 87 precincts reporting, but those totals included no early or absentee votes. The county asked to count about 900 absentees after the 8 p.m. deadline, and the state told it to count them as soon as possible. — [search summary of 11alive / WSAV coverage](https://11alive.com/article/news/verify/georgia-does-not-count-votes-before-election-day/85-dfb3c920-4519-473c-a915-f1f59eeb183a)
- **Fulton, 2020.** Officials sent most workers home around 10:30 p.m. A few counted until 1 a.m. with a state observer. This became the basis of later fraud claims. — [Law Officer recap](https://www.lawofficer.com/fulton-county-election-officials-counted-ballots-for-hours-on-election-night-after-telling-media-and-observers-they-were-shutting-down/)
- **2020, late counties.** As of 10:30 p.m. Thursday Nov 5, 2020, the largest remaining batches were Gwinnett (4,800) and Clayton (4,355), with 14,097 votes left statewide. — [CBS live blog](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/live-updates/georgia-2020-election-results)
- The Secretary of State counts turnout across "our 159 county election officials". — [Fox 5, Oct 20 2026](https://www.fox5atlanta.com/news/georgia-2026-elections-more-than-1m-georgians-have-voted-early)

### Inferences
- **Order of reporting:**
  1. Small rural counties report everything early, which leans R.
  2. Large metro counties (Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb, Gwinnett, Clayton) post big early/absentee batches near 8 p.m. These lean D, so Bottoms may lead in the early returns.
  3. Rural Election Day precincts then flow in (R-leaning), so Jackson closes.
  4. Metro Election Day precincts arrive last, after 11 p.m.
- That is the pattern described by AP's 2022 and 2024 call rationales: outstanding votes concentrated in "Democratic strongholds" or "Democratic areas" (section 5). The direction of late swings depends on which counties are still out, not on vote method alone.
- **Display glitches.** "Precincts reporting" can be misleading (Chatham 2024). Track expected vote, not precinct counts.

### Gaps
- No county-by-county 2024 or 2022 reporting timestamps found.
- Did not confirm that results.sos.ga.gov redirects to Enhanced Voting for the 2026 general. It is likely, given the 2026 primary used it.

## 5. Historical timing, shifts and calls (2018–2024)

### Takeaway
Before SB 189, Georgia counts dragged on for days (2018 and 2020). With the 2021–24 laws in place, races with clear margins were called within about 5–6 hours of poll close:
- 2022 Kemp: AP called at 12:45 a.m. (Abrams conceded around 11 p.m.).
- 2022 Warnock runoff: called late runoff night.
- 2024 Trump: AP called at 12:58 a.m. at 50.8%.

Razor-thin races still wait: 2020 president (AP call Nov 19, after the hand audit) and 2021 Ossoff (called the afternoon after).

### Cited Findings
- **2018 Gov (Kemp vs. Abrams):**
  - Unofficial returns showed Kemp at 50.3%.
  - Abrams refused to concede on election night (Nov 6) because absentee and provisional ballots were still out. Her campaign estimated a net 17,759 votes would force a Dec 4 runoff.
  - A federal judge barred certification before Friday 5 p.m.
  - Abrams ended her campaign Nov 16, 2018, 10 days after the election.
  - Sources: [Red & Black/AP](https://www.redandblack.com/athensnews/abrams-ends-race-for-governor-makes-plans-to-file-lawsuit/article_9efd3a18-e9f1-11e8-8e95-03e06d182721.html); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/dems-doubt-kemp-claims-insurmountable-lead-georgia-federal-judge-rules-n936506); [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/edlection2018-abrams-waits-on-uncounted-ballots-in-georgia-governors-race/)
- **2020 President:**
  - Trump led on election night, with a blue shift as absentee and metro ballots were counted.
  - By about 10:30 p.m. Thursday Nov 5, Trump's lead was down to 1,775 with 14,097 votes left. Biden passed him late Nov 5 / early Nov 6.
  - The hand audit ran from Nov 13 and finished Nov 19. Biden's margin was 12,284 in the hand count vs. 12,780 in the machine count.
  - AP called Georgia for Biden about 30 minutes after the audit ended, on Nov 19.
  - Sources: [CBS live blog](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/live-updates/georgia-2020-election-results); [Moultrie Observer, Nov 19 2020](https://moultrieobserver.com/2020/11/19/update-unprecedented-statewide-audit-reaffirms-biden-victory-in-georgia); [WABE](https://wabe.org/?p=83944)
- **2020 Senate:** AP declared the Perdue–Ossoff race headed to a runoff (Perdue just under 50%). — [WGXA](https://wgxa.tv/news/local/ap-runoff-declared-for-ossoff-and-perdues-race-for-georgia-senate)

  - AP declared Warnock the winner in the early hours of Jan 6, in a report updated at 2:45 a.m.
  - Ossoff was projected on Wednesday afternoon, Jan 6, as protesters stormed the Capitol.
  - Source: [WRDW](https://www.wrdw.com/2021/01/06/georgia-senate-runoffs-where-do-we-stand-as-votes-continue-to-come-in)

- **2022 Senate:**
  - Warnock led Walker by about 37,000 of about 4M votes but fell short of a majority, so the race went to a Dec 6 runoff.
  - Runoff: AP called for Warnock "late Tuesday" (Dec 6), since the uncounted ballots were mostly in Democratic areas. Final result was 51.4–48.6.

- **2024 President:** AP called Georgia for Trump at **12:58 a.m. ET Wed Nov 6**, with Trump at 50.8%. Harris would have needed 56.1% of the remaining vote. — [PBS/AP race call](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/ap-race-call-donald-trump-wins-georgia); [Local10/AP](https://local10.com/news/politics/2024/11/06/ap-race-call-donald-trump-wins-georgia)
- **2024 vote mix.** 3,761,968 early in-person plus 242,620 mail ballots, about 4.0M cast before Election Day. — [GA SoS press release](https://sos.ga.gov/news/georgia-voters-break-4-million-votes-during-early-voting-period)

### Inferences
- Under SB 189 (2024 onward), a governor race at about a 2-point margin (Kemp 2022 won by about 7.5; Trump 2024 by about 2.2) can be called around midnight to 1 a.m. ET. A race near 1 point or less likely waits for metro Election Day precincts, then late absentees, provisionals and UOCAVA ballots (through Nov 6), so a call could come Wednesday or later.
- The 2024 call at 12:58 a.m. with Trump at 50.8% is the best analog for a ~2-point race under the current law.
- **Shift direction:** The red-then-blue drift of 2020 has largely become **blue first (8 p.m. metro early votes), then red (Election Day precincts), then slightly blue at the end (last metro Election Day precincts and late absentees)**. This is an inference from the law's reporting order and the AP rationales above. Magnitude is not quantified here.

- Exact AP timestamps for the 2021 Ossoff and 2022 Warnock runoff calls were not found.

Election Day votes likely lean R. This is consistent with 2022 runoff reporting, but the magnitude is unquantified.
