# Kansas: vote counting, reporting order and results timing for the Nov 3, 2026 general (U.S. Senate: Marshall R-inc vs. Hamilton D)

Research date: 2026-10-21. Status labels: CONFIRMED = cited source; INFERENCE = my reasoning; GAP = not verified.

## 1. 2026 rules: poll closing, mail-ballot receipt deadline, processing, provisionals, canvass, certification, cure, recount

### Takeaway
**Headline: the three-day mail grace period is back in force for Nov 3, 2026.** SB 4 (2025, passed over a veto) ended it as of Jan 1, 2026. A Douglas County court then blocked SB 4 by injunction on July 16, 2026, and the appeals courts have refused to lift that injunction. So mail ballots postmarked by 7 p.m. Election Day still count if they arrive by the last mail delivery on **Friday Nov 6, 2026**. This could still change before Nov 3.

Other key times:
- Polls close at 8 p.m. ET in most of the state and 9 p.m. ET in the four Mountain-time counties.
- County canvasses fall on Monday Nov 9, and no later than Nov 16.
- State certification has historically come in early December.
- Kansas has no automatic recount.

### Cited Findings
**Poll hours (K.S.A. 25-106)**
- **Central-time counties:** polls open 7:00 a.m. and close 7:00 p.m. by default. The county election officer may change the hours by public announcement. Polls must stay open at least 12 continuous hours, cannot open before 6 a.m., cannot close after 8 p.m., and cannot close before 7 p.m. — [K.S.A. 25-106, ksrevisor.gov](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch25/025_001_0006.html)
- **Mountain-time counties:** the default is 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. MT. Polls cannot close after 7 p.m. MT or before 6 p.m. MT — [K.S.A. 25-106](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch25/025_001_0006.html)
- **AP's 2024 guidance:** polls close at 7 p.m. local, which is 8 p.m. ET for most of the state. Polls in Mountain time close at 9 p.m. ET, which implies those counties use the 7 p.m. MT maximum — [AP Decision Notes via AOL](https://www.aol.com/ap-decision-notes-expect-kansas-221653746.html)
- **Which counties:** four Kansas counties are in Mountain time, according to news guides — [KSNT](https://digital-release.ksnt.com/elections/what-time-do-the-polls-close-in-my-state/amp/)
  - The specific counties (Greeley, Hamilton, Sherman, Wallace) were not confirmed by a primary source in this session.

**The 2025 law (SB 4) and the 2026 litigation**
- **The law:** SB 4 ended the grace period. The Legislature overrode Gov. Kelly's veto on March 25, 2025, by 84-41 in the House and 30-10 in the Senate — [WIBW](https://www.wibw.com/2025/03/25/ks-legislature-overrides-veto-pushes-bill-through-eliminate-mail-ballot-grace-period)
- **Effective date:** SB 4 took effect Jan. 1, 2026 — [KCTV5](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/07/31/kansas-supreme-court-upholds-mail-ballot-grace-period-ahead-primary-election/)
- **Current statute text:** K.S.A. 25-1132(b) (2024 compilation, as amended) sets the mail receipt deadline at "7:00 p.m. on the date of the election" — [K.S.A. 25-1132, kslegislature.gov 2024](https://www.kslegislature.gov/li_2024/b2023_24/statute/025_000_0000_chapter/025_011_0000_article/025_011_0032_section/025_011_0032_k/)
- **Old rule (2017 law, used 2018–2024):** the mail deadline was "the last delivery of mail by the United States postal service on the third day following the date of the election." The ballot had to be postmarked, or otherwise shown by USPS to have been mailed, on or before the close of polls — [K.S.A. 25-1132, 2022 version](https://www.kslegislature.gov/li_2022/b2021_22/statute/025_000_0000_chapter/025_011_0000_article/025_011_0032_section/025_011_0032_k/)
- **Who sued:** Kansas Appleseed, Loud Light, the Disability Rights Center of Kansas and three voters, in Douglas County District Court — [KCTV5](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/07/31/kansas-supreme-court-upholds-mail-ballot-grace-period-ahead-primary-election/)
  - Ballotpedia lists the casesearch case ID as 17629603 — [Ballotpedia News](https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/07/30/kansas-three-day-grace-period-for-mail-ballots-temporarily-reinstated-ahead-of-primary/)
- **Timeline of court rulings:**

- **Printed instructions:** ballots printed with the 7 p.m. Election Day deadline are not binding, because the injunction supersedes them — [KCTV5](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/07/31/kansas-supreme-court-upholds-mail-ballot-grace-period-ahead-primary-election/)
- **Status:** the underlying constitutional question is still undecided in district court — [KCTV5](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/07/31/kansas-supreme-court-upholds-mail-ballot-grace-period-ahead-primary-election/)

**Processing and reporting advance ballots**
- **Before Election Day:** Kansas election officials may open and process advance ballots before Election Day. That includes signature verification, opening envelopes and running ballots through tabulators. This is a news report; no statute was cited — [KSHB 2020](https://www.kshb.com/news/election-2020/election-guide/kansas-advance-ballot-law-speeds-up-election-results)
- **What election-night results include:** an SoS regulation defines "unofficial election night returns" to include advance ballots counted on election day. It excludes provisional or challenged ballots and advance ballots received after election day — [Kansas Register, Sept 28, 2023 (K.A.R. 7-36)](https://sos.ks.gov/publications/Register/Volume-42/Issues/Issue-39/09-28-23-51519.html)

**Signature cure**
- **Rule:** officials must try to contact a voter whose advance ballot is unsigned or whose signature does not match (K.S.A. 25-1124(b); K.A.R. 7-36-9) — [NCSL table 15](https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/table-15-states-with-signature-cure-processes); [K.A.R. 7-36-9](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/kansas/K-A-R-7-36-9)
- **Deadline:** cures are accepted until the start of the final county canvass — [NCSL](https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/table-15-states-with-signature-cure-processes)

**County canvass (K.S.A. 25-3104)**
- **Timing rule:** for a Tuesday election, the county board canvasses between 8 and 10 a.m. on the following Monday. The county election officer may move it to another business day, but no later than 13 days after the election — [K.S.A. 25-3104 summary](https://ebench-dev.greeninfo.org/state/kansas/statute/25-3104); [K.S.A. 25-3104 (2018 PDF)](https://www.kslegislature.gov/li_2018/m/statute/025_000_0000_chapter/025_031_0000_article/025_031_0004_section/025_031_0004_k.pdf)
- **For 2026 (INFERENCE from the rule):** Monday, Nov 9, 2026. The latest allowed is Monday, Nov 16, 2026.
- **Precedent:**
  - Johnson County's canvass for Nov 5, 2024 was set for Nov 15, 2024, the 10th day — [JoCo canvass notice](https://jocoelection.org/sites/default/files/2024-09/11-05-2024%20-%20Notice%20of%20Time%20and%20Place%20of%20Canvass.pdf)
  - Sedgwick County's Aug 4, 2026 primary canvass began Aug 12 and certified on Aug 17 — [Sedgwick County](https://www.sedgwickcounty.org/communications/news-releases/final-unofficial-election-night-results-for-2026-primary-election/)
- **Provisional ballots:** reviewed and counted at the canvass (Sedgwick, 2026 primary) — [Sedgwick County](https://www.sedgwickcounty.org/communications/news-releases/final-unofficial-election-night-results-for-2026-primary-election/)

**State certification**
- **Who:** the State Board of Canvassers is the governor, the secretary of state and the attorney general. In 2024 it certified the general election on Monday, Dec 2, 2024, under K.S.A. 25-3206 — [WIBW](https://www.wibw.com/2024/12/02/kansas-leaders-certify-2024-election-results/); [KS SoS release, Nov 19, 2024](https://kssos.org/media/press-releases/2024/11-19-24-state-board-of-canvassers-to-meet.html)
- **2024 audit and recount outcome:** two races fell within the 1% margin that triggers an audit, and no candidate requested a recount — [WIBW](https://www.wibw.com/2024/12/02/kansas-leaders-certify-2024-election-results/)

**Recount**
- Kansas has no automatic recount. Any candidate may request and pay for one regardless of margin. If the requester ends up the winner, the counties bear the cost — [AP Decision Notes 2024](https://www.aol.com/ap-decision-notes-expect-kansas-221653746.html)

### Inferences
- **Further rulings:** the Kansas Supreme Court has twice declined emergency relief, and the Court of Appeals declined again in October. A reversal before Nov 3 looks unlikely but is possible. The expedited appeal is pending.
- **If the injunction is lifted late:** a court could still lift it before Nov 6. Ballots arriving Nov 4–6 might then be segregated and litigated. Watch for that.
- **ET times:** DST ends Sunday Nov 1, 2026, so 7 p.m. CST = 8 p.m. EST, and 7 p.m. MST = 9 p.m. EST.

## 2. Reporting order, units, results site and slow counties

### Takeaway
Kansas reports by 105 counties. The first returns, roughly 8:20–8:30 p.m. ET, are typically advance ballots (early in-person plus mail received before Election Day).

Mail ballots dropped off on Election Day are often added in later updates, the next day in Sedgwick. Late-arriving postmarked mail (through Fri Nov 6) and provisionals come in during the canvass the following week.

Johnson County is the swing-weight county and has historically reported quickly, but it has had blowups (2018 primary).

### Cited Findings
- **Reporting units:** results are certified by each of the 105 Kansas counties — [WIBW](https://www.wibw.com/2024/12/02/kansas-leaders-certify-2024-election-results/)
- **Mail results on election night:** about half of Kansas counties release mail-voting results along with in-person Election Day results over the course of the night — [AP Decision Notes 2024](https://www.aol.com/ap-decision-notes-expect-kansas-221653746.html)
- **AP timing benchmark (2022 Democratic governor primary):**

| Milestone | Time (ET) |
|---|---|
| First AP results | 8:24 p.m. |
| About half counted | ~10:02 p.m. |
| Last update of the night (~96% counted) | 3:31 a.m. |

  Source: [AP Decision Notes 2024](https://www.aol.com/ap-decision-notes-expect-kansas-221653746.html)
- **Johnson County:**
  - Its first unofficial partial results summary (early votes) is usually available by 7:30 p.m. CT, then updated periodically until all scanners report — [JoCo 2026 Precinct Committee Packet](https://www.jocoelection.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Precinct%20Committee%20Packet.pdf)
  - In 2024, Election Commissioner Fred Sherman said he would not post results while voters were still in line. He expected later batches around 8 p.m. and hoped for the last by 9 p.m. CT — [Johnson County Post](https://johnsoncountypost.com/?p=245959)
  - In 2020, Johnson County posted final unofficial results less than two hours after polls closed — [KSHB](https://www.kshb.com/news/election-2020/election-guide/kansas-advance-ballot-law-speeds-up-election-results)
  - In the August 2018 primary, a "huge influx" of advance ballots and a server crash pushed Johnson County results to the next afternoon — [KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/post/huge-influx-ballots-johnson-county-delays-election-results)
- **Sedgwick County (Wichita), March 2026 special:**
  - Election-night results included early in-person ballots, mail ballots and all Election Day in-person ballots.
  - A next-day update added mail ballots retrieved from drop boxes or polling places on Election Day.
  - Source: [KSHB / Sedgwick search summary](https://www.sedgwick.gov/communications/news-releases/final-unofficial-election-night-results-for-2026-wichita-special-election/)
- **Sedgwick County, Aug 2026 primary:** the same pattern. There was a Wednesday-afternoon update with drop-box and polling-place mail ballots, and provisionals were held for the canvass — [Sedgwick County](https://www.sedgwickcounty.org/communications/news-releases/final-unofficial-election-night-results-for-2026-primary-election/)
- **Key counties:** Johnson (most populous; Kansas City suburbs), Sedgwick (Wichita) and Shawnee (Topeka). Douglas (Lawrence) and Wyandotte (Kansas City, KS) are the most Democratic — [AP Decision Notes 2024](https://www.aol.com/ap-decision-notes-expect-kansas-221653746.html)

### Inferences
- **Early-lead direction:** advance votes lean D in Kansas, and the first dumps are advance-heavy from large urban counties (Johnson, Douglas, Wyandotte). The early count tends to be the most Democratic point of the night. Rural Election Day vote then moves it toward R.
  - Supported by 2020: Bollier led early, and Marshall finished ahead by more than 156,000 on election night (see section 4).
  - Supported by 2024 primary early-vote shares: 45% of the D primary vote was early versus 28% of the R.
- **Post-election additions:** drop-box ballots, grace-period mail and provisionals are probably modestly D-leaning, as in most states. This is not verified for Kansas.

### Gaps
- No county-level reporting-speed data for Wyandotte, Douglas or Shawnee in general elections.
- No exact URL or refresh cadence was confirmed for the SoS election night results site.

## 3. Law changes since 2022 affecting 2026

### Takeaway
The main change, SB 4 (no grace period), is enjoined for 2026. Separately, a USPS postmarking change and slow mail delivery increase the risk that ballots arrive late or carry late postmarks.

### Cited Findings
- **SB 4 (2025):** passed over Kelly's veto, effective Jan 1, 2026, and enjoined since July 16, 2026 — [KCTV5](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/07/31/kansas-supreme-court-upholds-mail-ballot-grace-period-ahead-primary-election/)
- **USPS postmarking:** USPS announced that in 2026 it will postmark mail when it is processed at regional facilities rather than when it is dropped off. A ballot mailed on time could carry a later postmark — [KSHB](https://www.kshb.com/news/local-news/kansas-election-law-and-usps-changes-means-voters-have-less-time-to-mail-ballots-in-2026)
- **Delivery performance:** in Q2 2025, only 51% of first-class mail in the Kansas-Missouri district was delivered on time — [KCTV5](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/07/31/kansas-supreme-court-upholds-mail-ballot-grace-period-ahead-primary-election/)
- **Rejections while SB 4 was in force:** in the March 3, 2026 Johnson County special election, about 1,008 of ~30,000 mail ballots (~1 in 30) were rejected for arriving after Election Day — [KCTV5](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/07/31/kansas-supreme-court-upholds-mail-ballot-grace-period-ahead-primary-election/)
- **Ballot mailing schedule:** mail ballots go out 20 days before the election (45 days for military and overseas voters) — [KSHB](https://www.kshb.com/news/local-news/kansas-election-law-and-usps-changes-means-voters-have-less-time-to-mail-ballots-in-2026)
  - Schwab sought arguments before Oct 14, the date mail ballots go out — [HPPR](https://www.hppr.org/hppr-news/2026-10-07/appeals-court-kansas-mail-in-ballots-will-be-accepted-for-three-days-after-november-election)
- **Regulation:** an SoS regulation (Kansas Register, Sept 2023) defines unofficial election-night returns — [Kansas Register](https://sos.ks.gov/publications/Register/Volume-42/Issues/Issue-39/09-28-23-51519.html)

### Inferences
- If postmarks are applied later, some ballots mailed on Election Day may be postmarked Nov 4 and rejected even within the grace window. That would modestly shrink grace-period volume relative to 2024.

### Gaps
- I did not inventory other 2023–2026 election bills (drop-box limits, ballot collection rules, other advance-voting changes). Only SB 4 and SB 394 (status unknown) surfaced.

## 4. Historical patterns 2018–2024

### Takeaway
Comfortable statewide races are called on election night:
- 2020 Senate: just after 10 p.m., most likely ET.
- 2024 President: likely at poll close; not verified.

Close races take until midday Wednesday:
- 2022 Governor: about a 2-point margin, called by AP around 12:30 p.m. CT Wednesday.

Late-arriving grace-period mail is small, roughly 2,000–2,800 ballots statewide in 2024 out of 1.34 million votes. That is about 0.2%, which only matters in a race inside about 0.3 points.

### Cited Findings
**2018 Governor (Kelly D vs. Kobach R)**

- **2020 call time:** "shortly after 10 p.m." is most likely ET, about 9 p.m. CT. That is consistent with the officials' 10:30 p.m. expectation and the 52%-in figure.
- **Final margins from memory, not verified this session:**

  - 2022 Governor: Kelly ~49.5–47.3.
  - 2024: Trump ~57–41.
- **2026 Senate scenarios:**
  - If Marshall wins by more than ~5 points, an AP call between ~9:30 and 11:30 p.m. ET is likely once rural Election Day vote offsets the D-leaning advance dumps.
  - If the margin is within ~2–3 points, expect the 2022 pattern: no AP call until Wednesday midday or later, waiting on Johnson/Sedgwick drop-box ballots and late-mail estimates.

- **Early-lead trap:** an early Hamilton (D) lead around 8:30–9:30 p.m. ET would be consistent with past patterns (Bollier 2020) and should not be read as a D win signal by itself.
- **Wednesday updates:** they are likely to be modestly D-leaning, because they are urban drop-box mail.
