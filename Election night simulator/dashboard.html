How to read this
  • Projected final: projector v1 (county by county) where NYT's counties line up with the benchmarks; otherwise v0 (statewide history). It starts from the markets' price at poll close, so any gap with the market comes from the count. The band is 80%.
  • Counted votes alone: what the counted votes point to with no starting point: each county's counted share against its usual lean, weighted toward counties that have counted most. Compare it with our model expected to see whether the votes are coming in over or under what we expected.
  • Over/under in the county table: each county's counted share against the share it gives if the state lands on our model's margin. Net votes turns that into votes: how far ahead (or behind) of that expectation the candidate is in the votes counted there. Tie needs is the share a tied statewide result needs from that county.
  • Model start: the same projection started from our margin model instead of the market.
  • FLIP: v1 gives a side 45% or more while the market prices it far lower, with 20%+ counted. That is the only kind of edge that paid in the 2024 test (Slotkin at 15c, Rosen at 33c). Check which counties are left and the book depth before acting. Every other best buy lost money in 2024: treat it as a prompt, not a trade.
  • Maine and Alaska are first-round counts; ranked-choice tabulation can still change the winner. Alaska reports by House district, so it has no county tracking. Not financial advice: METHODS.md has the weak spots.
