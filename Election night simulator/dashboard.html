Election Night 2026

Senate and governor races, projected county by county as the count comes in, against the prediction markets
How to read this
  • Projected final: our county-by-county projector, where NYT's counties line up with our benchmarks; otherwise the statewide projector. It starts from the markets' price when polls close, so any gap with the market comes from the count. The bar on the scale is its 80% range.
  • Counted votes vs model: what the votes counted so far point to on their own (each county's share against its usual lean, weighted toward counties further along), compared with our model's margin. Over means the Democrat (or Osborn in Nebraska) is doing better than our model expected; under means worse. Net votes puts that in votes.
  • County breakdown (click a race): each county's share of the state vote, the share our model expects there, the share a tied statewide result needs, and once it reports, its counted share, over/under in points and net votes, and votes left.
  • Win chance: the projector; the same projection started from our margin model; and the market (the middle of the Kalshi and Polymarket books).
  • FLIP: the projector gives a side 45% or more while the market prices it far lower, with 20%+ counted. It is the only kind of edge that paid in the 2024 test (Slotkin at 15c, Rosen at 33c). Check which counties are left and the book depth before acting. Every other best buy lost money in 2024: treat it as a prompt, not a trade.
  • Maine and Alaska are first-round counts; ranked-choice tabulation can still change the winner. Alaska reports by House district, so its breakdown drops out once results arrive. Not financial advice.
